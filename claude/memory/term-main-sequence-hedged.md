Write "main sequence" (English) instead of 主系列星, "predict logg" instead of 予測 logg / 予測した logg, "reference の星" instead of 参照の星, "giant" instead of 巨星, "Extinction" instead of 減光, and "spec2label cut" (never bare "cut" or 予測ラベル cut) for the Spec2Labels predicted Teff/logg selection, in Notion/README/figures (user, 2026-10-27). Stars selected only by an APOGEE logg/Teff region must be hedged: "main sequence らしき星", "main sequence と仮定した星"; in figures "Dwarf-like stars", not "Dwarfs".

**Why:** 2026-10-27 the user said the logg ≥ 4.2 box stars are not confirmed dwarfs ("Dwarfsと決まったわけではない") and asked for 主系列星 → main sequence.

**How to apply:** figure titles/labels and Notion text for [[xgb-rgb-analysis-plan]] and later analyses.
